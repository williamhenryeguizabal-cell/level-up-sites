Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with Level Up Sites.

How long does it take to develop a website?

Development time depends on the plan and complexity of the project. A basic website can be ready in 5 to 7 business days. A professional site with more sections, custom features, or an online store can take between 2 and 4 weeks.

The timeline starts officially once we receive all the necessary information: texts, images, colors, and design preferences.

Do I need to pay for hosting or domain separately?

Not necessarily. We can help you register the right domain (your internet address, e.g., yourbusiness.com) and web hosting for your project, or manage them for you if you prefer. We'll explain the costs before we begin.

Can I modify my website after delivery?

Yes. Once the site is delivered, you have full control over it. If the site was developed with an administration panel, you can edit text, images, and content without any technical knowledge.

If you need more extensive modifications, such as adding new sections, changing the design, or integrating new features, we can quote you for those changes separately.

What does the basic plan include?

The basic plan includes: custom design, up to 5 sections (home, services, about us, gallery and contact), WhatsApp button, responsive design for mobiles and tablets, and online publication.

It is ideal for businesses that need a professional web presence without complex functionalities: clinics, beauty salons, small restaurants, freelancers and entrepreneurs.

What does the professional plan include?

The professional plan includes everything in the basic plan plus: premium design with a custom visual identity, a contact form, up to 10 sections, animations, optimized basic SEO, social media integration, Google Maps integration, optimized loading speed, and extended support.

It can also include additional sections such as a blog, product catalog, booking system, or special modules depending on your business.

Can I request additional changes after the project is completed?

Of course. Any additional changes outside the original scope, such as adding new pages, redesigning sections or integrating new tools, are quoted separately based on the type and volume of the work.

We offer monthly maintenance plans for clients who need frequent updates. Contact us and we'll prepare a proposal tailored to your needs.
